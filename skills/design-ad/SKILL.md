# Design ad

Think like an expert designer. The ad must be on-brand (Branding section of the product's `-research.md`), instantly scannable, and legible at thumbnail size in a feed.

## Decide, per ad

- **Colors** — start from the product and packaging colors. Pick on-brand or near-on-brand backgrounds that give the product strong contrast, never one that flattens it.
- **Fonts** — consistent with the brand. Always write the word "font" when naming one (e.g. "bold grotesque sans font").
- **Spacing & rhythm** — consistent margins and gaps; nothing crowded, nothing adrift. For portrait feed creatives, describe each shot as five equal horizontal bands, numbered 1 at the bottom through 5 at the top, rather than percentage coordinates. Keep essential action and text in the middle three so they survive feed crops; specify subject and overlay bands separately when needed. Apply this to each storyboard frame, not the storyboard sheet as a whole.
- **Hierarchy** — the eye lands on the hook, then the product, then supporting text; size, weight, and position encode the order. One clear focal path.
- **Contrast & emphasis** — every text legible over its background. On a long hook, highlight the key word(s) — accent color, heavier weight, a marker swash or lozenge.
- **Assortment coverage** — a variety or multi-pack shows the full set somewhere: as the hero, in an explaining graphic, or in the footer band. Never a single unit.

## Text over photo

Don't just recolor the whole line. Use an accent color on the key word(s), a highlighter or marker swash, an underline, a solid lozenge or knockout panel, heavier weight, or shadows, strokes, gradients, or scrims. Place text over the calmest part of the photo.

On UGC and lifestyle photos, make overlays **social-organic** — native story-editor text blocks, stickers, slightly imperfect placement — so the ad reads as a post, not a banner.

## Layers that explain an idea

Charts, diagrams, timelines, cycles, processes, tables: few elements, clear labels, generous spacing. If it needs study, simplify it.

## Output

State the concrete choices (hex colors, font descriptions, spacing, hierarchy order, legibility treatments) for the **Design** section of the prompt. Where a creative choice is open, offer 3 differentiated directions.
