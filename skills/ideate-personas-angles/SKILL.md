# Ideate personas and angles

Each lives in a table beside the product's file: `inputs/products/<product>-personas.md` and
`<product>-angles.md`. **Seed 3 of each during research**, then append the chosen one whenever an ad
calls for a new one.

**Awareness stages:** unaware · problem-aware · solution-aware · product-aware · most-aware.

## Personas

One specific person an ad targets, grounded in `customers.md` and the market (`brand.md`), never "everyone".

| name | age | gender | awareness stage | physical pain | emotional pain | desired outcome | objection |

- **name** — 2–4 word descriptor (`sugar-alternatives-seeker`)
- **age** — a specific number, no ranges
- **gender** — female or male
- **awareness stage** — the stage + short note ("solution aware, knows there are several double sit-stand strollers")
- **physical pain** — third person ("leaks when she sneezes")
- **emotional pain** — how it feels ("fears she'll embarrass herself in front of others")
- **desired outcome** — life with the pain gone ("one less thing to worry about when she's out")
- **objection** — what blocks belief in a fix ("am I at that age already?")

New options differ in awareness stage, pain, or life situation.

## Angles

Any way to sell the product to a persona — a feature, benefit, technical detail, market fact,
offer, season, or cultural moment; the problem itself and why it keeps happening; how the product
works; proof (results, counts, a standout review); the real culprit (what the customer has been
wrongly blaming, or the fix that's been letting them down); against the grain (the usual advice is
wrong); who they are (their identity or moment of life); head-to-head (versus the named alternative);
or a competitor gap. **One ad communicates one angle.** Sharpen each into a claim (e.g. "the
product has no X → red is the new X").

When the angle is how the product works, take it from the product file's "Why it works"
(**research-brand**).

| angle | what it is + why it's relevant | fits |

- **angle** — a 2–4 word name.
- **what it is + why it's relevant** — one line: the claim, and why it lands for the persona.
- **fits** — the awareness stage(s) it suits, e.g. "problem-aware" or "product-aware +". Batches
  mix stages.

New options differ in source (e.g. one feature-led, one pain-led, one offer/seasonal).
