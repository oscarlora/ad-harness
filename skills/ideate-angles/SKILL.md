# Ideate angles

An angle is any way to sell the product to a persona — a feature, benefit, technical detail, market
fact, offer, season, or cultural moment; the problem itself and why it keeps happening; how the
product works; proof (results, counts, a standout review); the real culprit (the thing the customer
has been blaming wrongly, or the fix that's been letting her down); against the grain (the usual
advice is wrong, or the accepted fix backfires); who she is (the ad names her identity or moment of
life); head-to-head (versus the named alternative); or a competitor gap. **One ad communicates one
angle.** Sharpen each into a claim (e.g. "the product has no X → red is the new X").

When the angle is how the product works, state it as two short sentences — the real reason the
problem keeps happening, and why this product fixes exactly that. If a customer couldn't retell both
to a friend after one read, simplify or drop the angle. (The research doc's "Why it works" section is
the source when it exists.)

Angles live in the product's `[product]-angles.md`, a living table: **seed ≥5 during research**, then
append new ones as ads call for them.

## The table

| angle | what it is + why it's relevant | fits |

- **angle** — a 2–4 word name.
- **what it is + why it's relevant** — one line: the claim, and why it lands for the persona.
- **fits** — which awareness stage(s) of the personas this angle suits (unaware · problem-aware ·
  solution-aware · product-aware · most-aware, as used in `[product]-personas.md`). One short value,
  e.g. "problem-aware" or "product-aware +".

Batches should mix stages so no batch speaks to only one.

## Ideating new ones

When a new ad needs an angle, propose **3 deliberately differentiated** ones (different source — e.g.
one feature-led, one pain-led, one offer/seasonal), then append the chosen one to
`[product]-angles.md` so the set grows.
