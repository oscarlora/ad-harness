# Ideate angles

An angle is any way to sell the product to a persona — a feature, benefit, technical detail, market
fact, offer, season, or cultural moment; the problem itself and why it keeps happening; how the
product works; proof (results, counts, a standout review); the real culprit (what the customer has
been wrongly blaming, or the fix that's been letting her down); against the grain (the usual advice
is wrong); who she is (her identity or moment of life); head-to-head (versus the named alternative);
or a competitor gap. **One ad communicates one angle.** Sharpen each into a claim (e.g. "the product
has no X → red is the new X").

When the angle is how the product works, state it as two short sentences — why the problem keeps
happening, and why this product fixes exactly that (source: the research doc's "Why it works"). If a
customer couldn't retell both after one read, simplify or drop it.

Angles live in `[product]-angles.md`: **seed ≥5 during research**, then append as ads call for them.

## The table

| angle | what it is + why it's relevant | fits |

- **angle** — a 2–4 word name.
- **what it is + why it's relevant** — one line: the claim, and why it lands for the persona.
- **fits** — the awareness stage(s) it suits (unaware · problem-aware · solution-aware ·
  product-aware · most-aware), e.g. "problem-aware" or "product-aware +".

Batches mix stages.

## Ideating new ones

Propose **3 deliberately differentiated** angles (different source — e.g. one feature-led, one
pain-led, one offer/seasonal), then append the chosen one to `[product]-angles.md`.
