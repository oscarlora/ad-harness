Welcome to the Ad Production Kit (ADPK). You're all set.

ADPK turns your AI agent into an ad team for Meta ads, image and video. An ad gets made in three steps:
  1. Research: the agent reads your brand's website and reviews to learn what you sell, who buys it, and why.
  2. Ideas: it asks a few short questions and offers 3 ad ideas. You pick one.
  3. Make: it makes the ad in the Studio, an app that opens in your browser, where you watch each step
     land and leave notes.

To start, say: "Make an image ad for <your brand's website>".
