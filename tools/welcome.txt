Welcome to the Ad Production Kit — you're set up, and no API keys are needed yet.

What it is: everything you need to make Meta ads for your brand by talking to
your AI agent in plain words. You say what you want; the agent does the work and
shows you each step for approval.

How making an ad goes:
  1. Research — the agent reads your site and reviews: the product, who buys it, why.
  2. Concept — it offers 3 different ideas; you pick one.
  3. Write — hooks, script, on-screen copy.
  4. Make — images, voices, music, clips; you approve each step.
  5. Edit — cut together with captions; you click the video to leave notes.
  6. Review — finished ads land in the Studio for your yes.

The Studio is the kit's web app on your own computer: the Board (review ads),
Media (search your footage and images), and Workflow (each ad's progress and edit).
Your agent opens it for you — you just watch and click.

Keys come later: when you're ready to generate images or video, your agent walks
you through adding them then. For now, just tell it your brand and what you want
to make.
